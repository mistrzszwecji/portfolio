Design system · The platform the AI runs on · 2023-24

Built the Foundation. Then Rebuilt the Core.

I inherited an enterprise platform built by engineers - no design system, no consistent visual language. I built the foundation first, then the platform's new core module on top of it. The research I ran killed my own hypothesis for that module: management wants control over structure, teams want ownership over delivery. So I rebuilt the model, not the interface - and the system underneath is what made that affordable.

  • Initiatives became the platform's core - the company repositioned the product around it
  • Quarterly NPS up through the redesign, dipped after V1, recovered after the pivot
